About

I work on data-driven decision-making in consumer and small-business credit. Over about seven years at Ant Group, ByteDance and Du Xiaoman Financial, I have designed large-scale pricing experiments, built and refit machine-learning models for newly launched customer segments, worked on network-based transaction modeling, and developed fraud detection models, all deployed in production credit systems.

Selected Projects

Platform-wide pricing experiment and risk-based pricing

Ant Group · Experimental design & causal inference

Designed an unbiased, statistically powered, platform-wide price-discount experiment to estimate segment-level discount ROI (lifecycle × risk tier × creditworthiness tier) and locate its inflection point. Used the results to build a risk-based price-increase policy for price-insensitive users that offsets margin lost to discounts, and defined requirements for price-response models.

Cold start for a new lending segment

Ant Group · Data enrichment & operator identification

Built the credit strategy for a new lending vertical: lithium-battery purchase financing for heavy-truck drivers, a small segment that lacked business-operation signals (identity, business cash flow, operating capability). Combined manufacturer-provided data with internal risk data, identified self-employed and small-business operators, and mined features and refit models for the new population. The strategy raised approval rate and full-line coverage while holding risk stable.

Supply-demand network modeling for offline transactions

Ant Group · Network-based modeling

Modeled supply-demand networks to assess whether transaction needs are plausible in higher-risk offline merchant settings, and used this to raise full-line coverage only for selected transactions. Used AI to identify, generate and match network structures, run model refits, and condense the results into deployable decision rules that were put into production.

Risk × creditworthiness framework for SME credit-line decisions

ByteDance · Decision modeling under risk constraints

Designed a two-dimensional risk × creditworthiness framework for credit-line decisions in SME lending, with channel-specific eligibility boundaries and incremental offer optimization. With risk constraints held, approval rate rose 50% (relative), credit lines doubled, and risk fell 20%.

Credit-validity-extension field experiment

Du Xiaoman Financial · Field experiment

Designed an experiment that moved risk screening to the transaction stage to address data staleness and adverse selection when re-approving expired customers. Risk fell 5% and utilization rose 10%.

Telecom-fraud detection models

Du Xiaoman Financial · Fraud detection & imbalanced classification

Developed three generations of telecom-fraud detection models from scratch, covering basic scam patterns, large-loan differentiation and real-time features, reaching 90%+ fraud recall and cutting fraud incidence to 0.01%. The project won the company Innovation Award two quarters in a row.
