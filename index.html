Selected Projects

Platform-wide pricing experiment and risk-based pricing

Ant Group · Experimental design & causal inference

Designed an unbiased, statistically powered, platform-wide price-discount experiment to estimate segment-level discount ROI (lifecycle × risk × creditworthiness) and locate its inflection point, with AI-automated data collection and monitoring. Paired it with risk-based price increases for price-insensitive users that offset margin loss, managed against finance targets, and defined requirements for price-response models.

Cold start for a new lending segment

Ant Group · Data enrichment & operator identification

Built the credit strategy for a new vertical, lithium-battery financing for heavy-truck drivers: a small segment that had not been managed granularly and lacked business-operation signals (identity, business cash flow, operating capability). Fused manufacturer-provided and internal risk data, built self-employed / SME operator identification with AI-assisted feature mining, and refit models. The strategy raised approval rate and full-line coverage at stable risk.

Supply-demand network modeling for offline transactions

Ant Group · Network-based modeling

Used AI to identify, generate and match supply-demand networks that test whether transaction needs are plausible in higher-risk offline merchant settings, and compressed them into deployable decision rules backed by optimal model refits. The method is now in production and selectively raises full-line coverage.

Constrained approval and credit-line optimization for SME loans

ByteDance · Decision-making under a risk constraint · Led a 2-person virtual team

Raised approval rate and credit line together under a fixed risk constraint. Built a two-dimensional risk × creditworthiness line framework with channel-specific eligibility boundaries, continuously onboarded SME and internal scenario data, optimized offers incrementally, and reallocated risk budget across channels. Approval rate +50% (relative), credit line 2x, monthly originations +30%, risk −20%, and balance 15x in one year.

Exposure allocation for merchant loans

ByteDance · Risk tiering & credit allocation · Led a 2-person virtual team

For a CNY 70B+ portfolio with 4M+ customers, decomposed the growth problem into data onboarding, risk × creditworthiness tiering, eligibility and line assignment; used external data to measure repayment capacity and allocated exposure by cycle, competitiveness and customer response. Segment risk −30%, outstanding balance 2x in one year, average line +CNY 50K, and risk on CNY 500K+ exposures at 0.25x the portfolio level.

Telecom-fraud detection

Du Xiaoman Financial · Built 0→1, grew to a 3-person team

Developed three generations of detection models (basic scam patterns, large-loan differentiation, real-time features) and mined 20+ highly predictive features from device and app-list data. Fraud recall 90%+, fraud incidence 0.01%, manual review volume ~67% lower with +30% recall, and two large organized fraud rings stopped. Company Innovation Award in two consecutive quarters.
